# License Strategy

ModelDock starts under the MIT License for broad adoption and simple contribution. MIT releases remain MIT forever; a later custom or source-available license cannot claw back rights already granted for old versions.

Before any future license change, maintainers should:

- Publish the rationale.
- Review contribution ownership and DCO or CLA needs.
- Avoid calling a source-available license OSI-open-source unless it qualifies.
- Preserve third-party license obligations.
- Avoid copying OpenWebUI, LibreChat, LiteLLM, or other project code unless license compatibility is documented.
